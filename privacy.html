Privacy Policy — RepoVault

Last updated: 2026-08-20 · Version: 2.8.0

What Data We Collect

RepoVault stores, on your device only, the items you explicitly save: repository details (name, URL, stars, forks, language, topics), page titles and URLs, text you save as snippets, prompts or notes, and archived page text for offline reading. It also stores your tags, statuses, priorities and per-item settings.

How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally in your browser’s local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. RepoVault has no servers and no accounts.

How Data Is Used

Stored data powers the extension’s own features: search, filters, statistics, version history, backups and release tracking.

Third-Party Services

The extension makes on-demand requests to the public GitHub API (api.github.com) to retrieve public repository metadata (stars, forks, latest release) for repositories you explicitly choose to track. These requests contain only the public repository identifier and are subject to GitHub’s Privacy Policy. GitHub is a registered trademark of GitHub, Inc.; this extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by GitHub, Inc.

Data Sharing

RepoVault does not sell, share, or transmit your saved data to any third party.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items or clear all data at any time from the dashboard, and export your data as JSON, HTML or Markdown.
